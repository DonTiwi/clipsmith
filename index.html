Clip Smith

Clip Smith is a desktop app for Windows that turns long videos into short vertical clips and helps their creator post them to their own YouTube and TikTok accounts.

Everything runs on the user's own computer. You paste a video link, Clip Smith finds the best moments, frames and captions them, and you choose which of your connected accounts to post each clip to — now, or at a time you pick.

Clip Smith signs in with the platforms' official login pages and only posts what you choose to post.
