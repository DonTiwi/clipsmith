Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Clip Smith is a desktop app that runs entirely on your own computer. We do not run servers that receive your data, and we do not collect, sell or share personal information.

What stays on your computer

  • Sign-in tokens for the YouTube and TikTok accounts you connect, encrypted with Windows' built-in protection.
  • Basic account details shown in the app (name, handle, profile picture, follower and view counts) and a history of what you posted.
  • The videos and clips you make.

What is sent, and where

Clip Smith talks only to YouTube (Google) and TikTok, and only to do what you ask: sign you in, read your account's basic info and post statistics, and upload the clips you choose to post. Their own privacy policies apply to that data.

Permissions we ask for

YouTube: upload videos and read your channel's basic information. TikTok: your basic profile and statistics, your video list, and uploading or posting videos you choose.

Removing your data

Disconnect an account in Clip Smith to delete its tokens from your computer and revoke access. You can also remove access in your Google account (myaccount.google.com/permissions) or in TikTok's settings. Uninstalling Clip Smith and deleting its data folder removes everything it stored.
